## v3.3 - 2026-09-24
- Corrected the choked-flow sizing. When the outlet pressure is at or below about 58% of the inlet (absolute), the tool now uses the standard critical-flow relationship, in which flow depends on inlet pressure only. Before, it asked for 1.6 to 2 times the Cv it should at typical HVAC steam pressures (for example 28 psig to 5 psig at 293 lb/hr: 6.93, now 4.24) and under-sized above about 115 psia. Results agree with the Watson McDaniel and Spirax Sarco sizing calculators to within about 2%, and the required Cv no longer jumps at the choke point.
- The required Cv for non-choked (subcritical) conditions is unchanged. The check of whether a valve can pass the design flow used the same too-low choked capacity, so at some lower inlet pressures the tool suggested a valve larger than needed; that is corrected too.
- The corrected flow output and the on-screen formulas use the same relationship.
- Added seven new help (?) icons on the results side of the Calculator, in English, Spanish, Portuguese and French: Flow Coefficient Result, the Selected Valve panel, Flow Regime, Required / Difference / Margin, Target Velocity, Suggested Valve Size & Cv, and Valve Travel %. They explain what each result means, why a large Cv margin is not necessarily a problem, and that Valve Travel % is an estimate from a generic equal-percentage curve.
- When the Suggested Valve differs from the valve chosen in the Valve Selection box, a note under the Suggested Valve panel now says why (for example, the closest-match valve fails the valve-port velocity limit, with its velocity against the limit, and which valve passes). Available in all four languages.

## v3.0 - 2026-09-15
- Redesigned how updates are installed: you now choose where to save the new version yourself through a standard Save As dialog, just like saving any other file - no more hidden install folder or Desktop shortcut to manage.
- Added a "Download Fresh Copy" button on the About screen. Use it any time you want a clean copy of the currently published version, even if the tool thinks you're already up to date.
- Fixed a couple of issues where update messages could show up blank or with garbled text instead of the intended wording.
- The About screen's info button is now easier to recognize and no longer sits next to the valve diagram, where it looked like a help button for the picture.
